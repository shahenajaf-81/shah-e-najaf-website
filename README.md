# Shah e Najaf Translation Centre & Business Law Associates

A responsive, bilingual (English/Urdu) static website with navy-blue and gold styling.

## Files
- `index.html` — page content and WhatsApp links
- `style.css` — layout, colors and responsive styles
- `script.js` — language toggle and current year

## Before launch
1. Verify the business phone/WhatsApp number.
2. Add the real office address, email, business hours and any relevant registrations only if you want them public.
3. Confirm which translation languages you actually offer and any certification/attestation requirements. The site intentionally avoids claiming embassy recognition or guaranteed visa acceptance.
4. Test all links on a phone.

## Low-cost publishing with GitHub Pages
1. Create/sign in to a GitHub account.
2. Create a new public repository, e.g. `shah-e-najaf-website`.
3. Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js` and `README.md` to the repository root.
4. Open **Settings → Pages**.
5. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then Save.
6. Wait for GitHub Pages to publish and use the URL shown on the Pages settings screen.
7. For a custom domain, buy a domain from a reputable registrar and follow GitHub Pages' current custom-domain instructions. Domain registration usually costs extra.
8. Do not upload client documents, CNICs, passports or private records to the public repository.

Alternative: use Netlify Drop by uploading the website folder/files in its current dashboard. Check its current free-plan limits and terms before using.

## Marketing starter plan (first 30 days)
- Week 1: Set up/verify Google Business Profile if eligible, add real business details, upload a logo and genuine office photos, and prepare a WhatsApp Business catalogue.
- Week 2: Publish 3 helpful posts: translation checklist, document-formatting service, and affidavit drafting/typing service. Avoid promising visa approval or embassy acceptance.
- Week 3: Contact relevant local businesses and travel/document consultants personally; ask permission before sending promotional messages.
- Week 4: Track inquiries, quotes, paid orders and expenses. Keep ad spending small until you know which service converts.

## Important
This is a static brochure website; it does not include online payments, file upload, client login, automated quote generation, analytics or a working contact form. WhatsApp buttons open a chat. For sensitive documents, agree on a safe submission method and retention policy first.
